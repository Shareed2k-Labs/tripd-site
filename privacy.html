tripd privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Who we are

tripd is an Android trip planner for groups, developed by an independent developer (Shareed2k). Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

Short version

  • There is no developer server and no account with the developer. The developer does not receive your trips, your name or your keys.
  • Your trip data lives on a tripd host: a phone of yours or of a trip member. You delete it there.
  • The app contains no analytics, no ads and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • Some features send data to third-party services. Each one is listed below, with what it receives and when.

Your trip data

Trips, stops, items, bookings, checklists, expenses, people and guides are stored on a tripd host running on your phone or on a trip member's phone. Phones of members sync with that host. The data never goes to the developer. A tripd identity lives in a host on your own phone or a friend's phone, together with its recovery key; the developer has no account for you and cannot see, recover or delete one.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and sees your IP address like any web server. A link such as "Open in Google" in chat opens your browser at your request.

City suggestions use an offline GeoNames database bundled in the app and send nothing.

Your control

In Settings, Weather, Guides (including "Update guides automatically"), Holidays and Routes each have a switch and a note saying what is sent. Turn a switch off and that service is not contacted. AI, search and S3 work only when you enter your own provider, key or address. The map uses OpenFreeMap whenever you view it. You can also choose the on-phone model so AI data stays on the phone.

Reporting an AI answer

Every AI answer in Ask has a "Report" action. A report you send goes to the developer's Google Form over HTTPS. It contains the reason, your optional note, the AI provider and model name, the app version, and the reported answer's text (up to 2,000 characters) when "Include the answer" is ticked, which is the default. A report you send may include the reported answer, which can contain details of your trip, such as names, bookings, money or places. The sheet shows exactly what will be sent and you can untick the box. No other trip data, keys, user name, position or chat history is sent.

Retention and deletion

The developer keeps no trip data. Your data stays on the hosts of you and your trip members; delete a trip, or clear the app, on those phones to remove it. Backups you make (to Downloads/tripd on the phone, or to your own S3) are yours to delete. Reports received through the form are kept only as long as needed to review the content and may be deleted at any time; write to the contact address to ask for a report to be deleted.

Children

tripd is not directed at children and is meant for people 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
